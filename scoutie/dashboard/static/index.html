What this demo is actually showing: Scoutie's reasoning engine — the constraint tracking, retrieval, and ranking underneath — not a polished conversational UI. Scoutie runs fully deterministically (no generative model in its decision path) against a frozen 50,000-item catalog, so questions are answered with buttons drawn from real values in the current candidate pool, rather than open free-form chat. That's a deliberate choice to keep every interaction grounded in what the engine can actually reason about, not a limitation we're hiding.
